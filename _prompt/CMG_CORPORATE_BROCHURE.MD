### INTERNATIONAL SHOOTING RANGE AND ENTERTAINMENT COMPLEX

#### Shooting Competition and Training Area

The shooting competition and training area is a professional area for conducting shooting sports competitions and daily training. It features various types of shooting ranges, such as clay pigeon shooting ranges, rifle ranges, pistol ranges, etc., equipped with professional target positions, trap systems, and electronic scoring equipment, which can accurately simulate different competition scenarios and meet the training and competition needs of various shooting events.

#### Arms Testing and Training Area

The arms testing and training area is equipped with weapon testing zones, heavy equipment parking areas, military physical training grounds, squad and platoon combat training grounds, military 800-meter training grounds, mobile equipment performance training grounds, logistics support areas, etc.

#### National Defense Education Expansion Area

The National Defense Education Expansion Area is a comprehensive facility integrating national defense education, military experience, and outdoor expansion functions. The design concept is to cultivate national defense military literacy, patriotic spirit, and historical mission through simulated military experiences.

#### Comprehensive Service Area

The comprehensive service area mainly serves reception and service support functions, including entrance reception service center, social vehicle parking lot, electric vehicle parking lot, etc.

The Laos CMG International Shooting Range project is a high-end shooting entertainment and professional training complex located in Vientiane, aiming to create a one-stop service venue integrating military professional training, military reception, and leisure relaxation.

**First Phase Development Area**: 270 hectares

### Detailed Facilities

#### 01. Indoor Shooting Venues

Indoor shooting venues mainly include shooting areas, equipment control areas, rest and observation areas, firearms and ammunition management areas, auxiliary functional areas, etc. According to different shooting events and audience needs, various types of target lanes can be set up, equipped with advanced shooting equipment, target reporting systems, and related supporting facilities to meet various functions such as training, competition, and experience.

#### 02. Shooting Training and Competition Dedicated Venues

Shooting competition and training dedicated venues, planned to include lengths of 10 meters, 25 meters, 50 meters, 100 meters, 200 meters, can meet the needs of various shooting training and competitions.

#### 03. Clay Pigeon Shooting Range

Clay pigeon shooting range can be divided into competition area, training area, and auxiliary facilities area. The competition area is set up with international standard target positions (such as multi-directional, bi-directional, double multi-directional clay pigeon target positions); auxiliary facilities area includes athlete rest area, referee area, spectator area, ammunition depot, equipment maintenance room, etc. It can meet the needs of professional competitions and national team training, and also provide shooting enthusiasts with simulated combat training environment, which is a professional shooting venue integrating competition, training, and experience.

#### 04. IPSC Competitive Shooting Dedicated Venue

IPSC on one hand refers to the International Practical Shooting Confederation, an international organization, on the other hand refers to the practical shooting sport it advocates, which is a shooting sport developed based on defensive shooting skills. IPSC competitive shooting dedicated venue mainly covers simulated combat scenario areas, safety control areas, spectator and waiting areas, firearms and ammunition management center, comprehensive support areas, and other core areas.
